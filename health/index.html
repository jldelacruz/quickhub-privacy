Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

QuickHealth Hub ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

QuickHealth Hub is designed to prioritize your privacy.

  • We do not collect personal data such as your full name, email address, or contact information.
  • The app may store optional user-entered information locally on your device, such as nickname, age, weight, water intake, steps, habits, sleep logs, and wellness-related progress data.
  • All app data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used only within the app to:

  • Provide wellness tracking features
  • Calculate wellness scores and insights
  • Track habits and progress
  • Improve your in-app experience

3. Data Storage and Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device and remains there until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app.

We do not store your data on external servers and we do not have access to your personal app data.

4. Data Deletion

You may delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app’s storage/data through your device settings
  • Using any in-app reset or clear data features (if available)
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Once deleted or uninstalled, your locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as advertising providers (for example, Google AdMob).

These services may collect limited device-related information for advertising and analytics purposes.

Please refer to their privacy policy for more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect your information. However, since data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for securing access to your device.

7. Children’s Privacy

QuickHealth Hub is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
