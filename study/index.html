Privacy Policy for QuickStudy Hub

Last updated: May 2026

QuickStudy Hub ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

QuickStudy Hub is designed to work offline-first. We do not require account creation or collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or phone number.

We may store the following locally on your device:

  • Study sets and flashcards
  • Quiz progress and scores
  • Study streaks and daily goals
  • App settings and preferences

2. Local Storage

All study data is stored locally on your device using secure storage (such as AsyncStorage). This data is not uploaded to any external server unless explicitly stated in future updates.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use limited data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

AdMob may collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App usage data for ad personalization

For more details, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Analytics & Performance Tracking

We may collect anonymous usage data such as:

  • Feature usage (e.g., flashcards, quizzes, timers)
  • Session duration
  • App interactions (non-personal)

This data helps us improve app performance and user experience.

5. Notifications

The app may send local notifications such as study reminders and streak alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in device settings or within the app.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over your information. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

7. Children’s Privacy

QuickStudy Hub does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The app is safe for general educational use.

8. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)

These services may have their own privacy policies.

By using QuickStudy Hub, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
