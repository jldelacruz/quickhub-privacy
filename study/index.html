Privacy Policy for QuickStudy Hub

Last updated: May 2026

QuickStudy Hub ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

We may store the following locally on your device:

  • Study sets and flashcards
  • Quiz results and progress
  • Study streaks and goals
  • App settings and preferences

2. Local Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using secure storage (e.g. AsyncStorage). We do not upload or sync your data to external servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect limited data such as device identifiers and usage signals to serve ads.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Analytics & Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage data such as feature usage and session duration to improve app performance and user experience.

5. Notifications

The app may send local reminders for study sessions and streak tracking. You can disable notifications anytime in settings.

6. Data Security

Your data stays on your device. We do not sell, transfer, or share personal data.

7. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data.

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Use the “Reset Data” option inside the app Settings
  • Uninstall the app from your device (this removes all locally stored data)

Once deleted, all study sets, progress, streaks, and settings will be permanently removed from your device.

8. Children’s Privacy

QuickStudy Hub does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (advertising)

10. Your Consent

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
