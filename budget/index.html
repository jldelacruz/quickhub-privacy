Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 6, 2026

QuickBudget Hub ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or share personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location data

2. Local Data Storage

QuickBudget Hub stores your app data locally on your device using secure local storage technologies. This may include:

  • Expenses and spending history
  • Budget settings
  • Preferences and notification settings
  • Quick-add amounts
  • App usage preferences

Your data is not uploaded to our servers and remains only on your device unless you manually delete it.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored locally on your device for as long as you continue using the app.

You may delete your data at any time by:

  • Using the “Reset Data” option available in the app settings
  • Clearing the app’s storage through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

4. Advertising

QuickBudget Hub may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks such as Google AdMob.

These providers may collect limited information including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising ID
  • App interaction data

This information is used to provide and improve relevant advertisements.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect your information. Because your data is stored locally on your device, you maintain control over your information.

6. Children’s Privacy

QuickBudget Hub is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices and Control

You may:

  • Modify your budget and preferences anytime
  • Delete your stored app data using in-app reset tools
  • Disable notifications through app or device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
